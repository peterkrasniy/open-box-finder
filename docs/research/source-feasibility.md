## Platform APIs are acquisition candidates, not merchant admission

[WooCommerce Store API](https://developer.woocommerce.com/docs/apis/store-api/) intentionally exposes unauthenticated customer-facing product reads, with pagination up to 100 items per page. Its [Products API](https://developer.woocommerce.com/docs/apis/store-api/resources-endpoints/products/) distinguishes published products, purchasability, stock and price currency/minor units. Merchant terms, actual endpoint behavior, open-box condition and media rights still require separate review. Skip protected/hidden products rather than treating API visibility as authority to republish them. Do not call cart/order/customer or write endpoints for this price-comparison task.

[Shopify Ajax Product API](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/ajax/reference/product) returns individual variant prices, availability, images and comparison fields; prices use the customer's presentment currency and locale-aware paths. Its documentation examples are not actual inventory. Product-level minimum price and maximum comparison price may belong to different variants: never combine them into an apparent discount. `requires_shipping` alone does not establish shipment eligibility to the user's address or exact store pickup. A platform endpoint does not override a merchant's collection/reuse restrictions.

[UpChannels storefront documentation](https://www.upchannels.com/services/website-development) describes individual open-box/scratch-and-dent units and removal of sold one-off units. [Platform terms](https://www.upchannels.com/legal/terms) leave submitted customer data rights with the merchant and do not grant an aggregator redistribution rights. This corroborates a possible physical-unit data model for the Sacramento lead; it does not clear that merchant, its photos or its acquisition path.

## Evidence discipline

Research tools may show cached crawls, client placeholders, simultaneous stock/notify labels, regional redirects, or search snippets. Treat these as candidates until a permitted actual inventory response establishes the item, price, fulfillment, and observation time. A successful page fetch is not an availability test. Proxy/network failures must be distinguished from retailer denials and from empty inventory.

Free public syndication can expose useful fresh deal posts without exposing retailer stock. That boundary must remain visible in the product and in completion reporting. Do not broaden feed rights into scraping linked retailer pages. Lack of an affirmative feed license alone is not a universal prohibition on factual price/model/availability comparisons: assess actual access, automation and contract restrictions separately from protected media/descriptions/logos.
