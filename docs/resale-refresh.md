# Checked resale observations

The owner’s configured daily 8am America/Los_Angeles research covers Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist and OfferUp. The app does not scrape them, authenticate to them, contact sellers, or execute this schedule. Research and permission checks belong to the external collection workflow. Access failures, challenges and permission restrictions are stop conditions, never bypassed.

`data/resale-records.json` stores public listing observations and separately labeled comparisons. Never include seller profiles, names, contacts, private addresses, account tokens or screenshots of private sessions. Preserve source URLs, exact asking prices, actual public listing photos, check time, model/bundle uncertainties and testing caveats. Facebook asking prices are not sold evidence. A related-family mean must not be described as an exact model, exact bundle or local-market realized average. Reverb Transaction History Final Price observations are sold evidence, separately labeled from eBay and Facebook asking prices. eBay Best Offer accepted prices that are hidden remain upper bounds.

Create a reviewed JSON batch with `records`, `archivedIds`, `failedSources` arrays. Partial checks must never infer removal from absence. Archive only explicitly withdrawn/unavailable IDs. Failed sources preserve the previous records and timestamps. Never refresh an evidence time just because a build ran. Estimates must remain conservative and state cash-sale costs, tax/labor exclusions, inspection requirements and uncertain demand.

Run `node scripts/refresh-resale-catalog.mjs --input /tmp/checked-resale.json` for a dry run. After reviewing the receipt, append `--apply`. The script validates records, scope, dates, duplicate IDs and source identity, preserves unaffected entries, and records checksums and changes in `data/resale-history.jsonl`. Routine publication has no minimum-count gate; honest removals and failures may reduce the catalog to zero. Keep saved IDs stable and archived records in the file.

Run typecheck, lint and tests, build/package through the Sites source workflow, sync the exact commit to the canonical GitHub source, and deploy with the existing owner audience. Neither the public client nor the read-only endpoint can edit records. The browser checks `/api/resale-catalog` with `X-Resale-Schema: 3` for price-basis and Reverb-aware records; v24/v25 schema 2 responses retain all their supported observations with new price metadata projected out; existing v23 pages receive their supported eBay-only records and need one reload to adopt the new schema. The browser checks the endpoint every minute while visible and on focus. Published additions, updates and archives appear without a page reload. A request failure preserves the last dataset and shows a warning. All listing observations expire after 24 hours even if refresh fails. Exact pickup coordinates are not available for these leads, so the resale UI explicitly does not verify radius or provide fabricated navigation.

Direct Tools retail collection remains permission-held under its official terms: https://www.directtoolsoutlet.com/policies/terms-of-service. Do not retry automatic or manual catalog copying without permission or an approved feed. Existing observations keep their old check times and expire normally.

The collapsed resale card shows asking price, conservative potential resale, observed brand-new amount or a clear unavailable/unverified status, reserve, and estimated profit. Profit uses resale minus asking minus reserve; new reference prices never drive it. `newPriceReference` may specify its own `checkedAt` and `match` (`exact-model`, `model-unverified`, or `different-bundle`). `newPriceStatus` records a sourced unverified, permanently-out-of-stock, or discontinued state without an invented amount. These metadata checks never renew the listing observation or seller availability. Historical catalog values stay inside details and are not current-new prices. Current-new references expire after 24 hours from their own check. Never label permanently out of stock as discontinued unless a source establishes discontinuation.

The owner's overnight goal of at least 50 new qualified resale observations by 8am Pacific is a research target, not a publication threshold. Import only verified qualified batches; keep uncertainty, held sellers, failed checks, honest expiry and source permission holds. A shortfall never authorizes padding, broader access, fabricated prices or extended timestamps.
